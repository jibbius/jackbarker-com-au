WhereTheTruck.At is a web and iPhone application that enables users to locate Gourmet Food vans across Australia. I started this project with two others (Tom Kinsman - business strategist; and Xavier Verhoeven - graphic designer).

I was responsible for all development of the website and web APIs.

Our successes included: the launch of a website; [crowd-funding campaign](https://www.pozible.com/project/13151){:target="_blank"}; iPhone app; significant press coverage; and sale of the business in 2015.
