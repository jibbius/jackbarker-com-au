## Achievement Overview

Multiple award winner at the 2017 GovHack Hackathon in Ballarat, Victoria. Our team "CityZen" developed CityZen.space, a comprehensive data comparison platform analyzing city versus regional living standards. The project won **6 different awards** across multiple categories, demonstrating exceptional technical and analytical capabilities.

**Project Link:** [CityZen.space on GovHack 2017](https://2017.hackerspace.govhack.org/project/cityzenspace)

## Awards Received

**Primary Awards:**
- **Commerce Ballarat Demographics** - "What makes Ballarat and where are the gaps?"
- **Ballarat Public Health & Wellbeing**
- **Ballarat Technology Park Creativity Challenge**

**Technical Excellence Awards:**
- **Censational Hack** - Best Use of Census Data
- **Bounty: .tech / .space** - Technical innovation recognition
- **Future of Work Hack** - Economic analysis and workforce insights

## Project: CityZen.space

**Project Scope**
A comprehensive comparison platform analyzing Ballarat versus Melbourne living standards, examining multiple quality-of-life factors through data visualization and statistical analysis.

**Data Analysis Focus**
- Housing affordability and rental market analysis
- Crime statistics comparison and safety metrics
- Employment rates and income distribution
- Quality of life indicators and regional advantages
- Demographic trends and population analysis

**Technical Implementation**
- Government dataset integration from multiple sources
- Advanced data visualization and interactive dashboards  
- Statistical analysis and comparative modeling
- User-friendly interface for public engagement
- Real-time data processing and presentation

**Datasets Utilized**
- Australian Bureau of Statistics Census data
- Victoria Police crime statistics
- Department of Housing affordability metrics
- Australian Taxation Office income data
- Regional development and employment statistics

## Innovation & Impact

**Technical Innovation**
Created intuitive data visualizations that made complex government datasets accessible to general public, enabling informed decision-making about regional versus metropolitan living.

**Community Value**

- Public policy development and resource allocation
