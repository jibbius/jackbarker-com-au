One of the largest IT projects in Melbourne Financial sector history, arising from AMP's acquisition of AXA in 2011. Responsible for delivering multiple functional and data migration specifications across the project lifecycle.

## Key Responsibilities

**Enterprise Data Warehouse Integration**
- Designed and implemented data integration strategies for merged organizations
- Ensured data consistency and integrity across legacy systems

**Migration of In-Flight Corporate Actions**
- Managed complex financial transaction migrations during system transitions
- Maintained business continuity for active corporate actions

**FATCA Compliance Implementation**
- Delivered compliance solutions for United States Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act
- Ensured regulatory compliance across merged platform

**Family Fee Aggregation**
- Implemented consolidated fee calculation and reporting across family accounts
- Streamlined billing processes for improved customer experience

## Technical Challenges

- Large-scale data migration across disparate legacy systems
- Maintaining business operations during platform consolidation
- Regulatory compliance during system transformation
- Complex financial product and fee structure integration

## Impact

Successfully contributed to one of Australia's most significant financial services mergers, ensuring smooth transition of customer accounts and regulatory compliance while maintaining operational continuity.
