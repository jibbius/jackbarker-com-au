Major platform migration project for Old Mutual Wealth (OMW), one of the UK's largest wealth management providers, to reduce cost-per-customer through 3rd party administrator introduction.

## Project Scope

The project involved replacing multiple legacy systems with the Bluedoor Wealth Management Platform and transitioning to a distributed, multi-organisation architecture. Central to this was the complete redesign of the Adviser web portal used by third-party financial advisers.

## Key Responsibilities

**Product Owner Role** for high complexity areas including:
- Customer onboarding processes
- Financial account setup and configuration  
- Contributions management (including fees and taxation)
- Pension products and administration

**Core Activities:**
- **Stakeholder Engagement** - Regular collaboration with UK-based teams and business users
- **Solution Design Documentation** - Comprehensive technical and functional specifications
- **User Story Production** - Agile development support and scrum team collaboration
- **Systems Interface Requirements** - Integration specifications for distributed architecture

## Technical Challenges

- Migration from legacy systems to modern platform architecture
- Multi-organization data and process integration
- Complex financial product configuration and compliance
- Distributed system architecture design and implementation

## Impact

Successfully delivered a modernized adviser portal that reduced operational costs while improving user experience for financial advisers managing customer accounts across the UK market.
